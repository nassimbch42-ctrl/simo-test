# Technician Manual Checklist

Software cannot verify every physical fault. During a complete service, manually verify:

- USB-A / USB-C physical insertion and data transfer
- HDMI / DisplayPort output
- Charging connector and charger cable
- Keyboard keys and backlight
- Touchpad clicks and gestures
- Touchscreen multi-touch
- Webcam image and microphone capture
- Speakers, headphone jack and microphone jack
- Dead/stuck pixels and display uniformity
- Hinge, chassis, screws and physical damage
- Fan noise and abnormal vibration
- Battery swelling
- Wi-Fi/Bluetooth range and stability
- Ethernet link with a known-good cable
- Storage SMART attributes using a trusted vendor/controller-aware tool when required
